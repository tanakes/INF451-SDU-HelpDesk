# Dormitory — Girls' House (Student House)

## Facilities
- Girls' dormitory occupies two buildings (3 and 4 floors) within the SDU
  Student House.
- Each floor has 20 rooms, 4 student places per room.
- Each floor is roughly 1000 square meters, including bathrooms and
  showers.
- Each floor has a room for on-duty student assistants.

## Safety & Operating Hours
- About 70 staff members support medical, electrical, plumbing, painting,
  cleaning, laundry, and administrative services, available 24 hours.
- Dormitory operating hours: **6:00 AM to 10:00 PM (22:00)**, with
  **lights-off at 11:00 PM (23:00)**.

## Social Life
Regular events throughout the year: welcome party, cinema nights, the
Dormitory football cup, Student House parties, mountain picnics,
workshops, and holiday celebrations.

## Required Documents (for admission to the dormitory)
- 2 photos, size 3×4 cm
- Copy of Medical Form No. 086
- Copy of National ID
- Copy of payment invoice

Documents should be sent to the relevant dormitory service email
(girls: dormservice.girls@mail.ru; boys: dormservice.boys@mail.ru /
dormserviceboys@sdu.edu.kz).
