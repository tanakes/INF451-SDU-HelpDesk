# Dormitory — SDU Student House (Overview)

SDU's Student House has **separate buildings for male and female students**:
Boys' Dormitory and Girls' Dormitory. Both buildings share the same general
facilities, operating hours, and required-documents list; they differ in
building location and dormitory-office contacts.

## Shared Facilities & Rules
- Each floor has 20 rooms with 4 student places per room, roughly 1000
  square meters per floor including bathrooms and showers.
- Each floor has a room for on-duty student assistants.
- About 70 staff members support medical, electrical, plumbing, painting,
  cleaning, laundry, and administrative services, available 24 hours.
- Dormitory operating hours: **6:00 AM to 10:00 PM (22:00)**, with
  **lights-off at 11:00 PM (23:00)**.
- Regular social events throughout the year: welcome party, cinema nights,
  the Dormitory football cup, Student House parties, mountain picnics,
  workshops, and holiday celebrations.

## Required Documents (same for both dormitories)
- 2 photos, size 3×4 cm
- Copy of Medical Form No. 086
- Copy of National ID
- Copy of payment invoice

## Which Dormitory Do You Need?
- Ask about the **Boys' Dormitory** for male-student building details and
  contacts.
- Ask about the **Girls' Dormitory** for female-student building details
  and contacts.

## Payment
Dormitory and related payments can be made online via Kaspi.kz. Official
wire-transfer bank details are published on the university's payment pages
(institution: Suleyman Demirel University; BIN 960240000550; SWIFT
HSBKKZKX; Halyk Bank of Kazakhstan).
