# Memo: Transfer & Reinstatement Credit Mapping

## Purpose
Clarifies how courses completed at a **college** (prior to transferring
into an SDU degree program) map onto SDU's curriculum, per clauses 6.5,
7.7, and 7.9 of the Transfer and Readmission Policy.

## Key Rules
- Academic differences in coursework are determined by comparing the
  student's college transcript, syllabi, and credit volume (ECTS)
  against the SDU curriculum.
- Credit transfer is based on comparing program content, course volume,
  and learning outcomes.
- **If the credit volume of a transferred course is insufficient, it is
  not transferred** (the student must retake it at SDU).

## Example Mappings (college → SDU course)
| College Course | SDU Course | ECTS |
|---|---|---|
| Information and Communications Technologies | Information and Communications Technologies (Computer Science) | 5 |
| Russian/Kazakh language and literature | Russian language 1-2 / Kazakh language 1-2 | 10 |
| Fundamentals of political science and sociology | Module of Social and Political Knowledge (Political Science/Sociology) | 2 each |
| Physical Education | Physical Education 1-4 | 8 |

- Students admitted to an **English-taught** program do not get "Foreign
  Language (English)" transferred — they must take the MDE Placement
  Test instead.
- Students admitted to a **Kazakh/Russian-taught** program have two
  compulsory English courses transferred based on their college
  transcript grades.
- "History of Kazakhstan" is a state exam course — always required
  regardless of transfer status.
