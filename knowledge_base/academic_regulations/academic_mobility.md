# Academic Mobility Regulation (ДП-SDU-24)

## What Is Academic Mobility?
The temporary transfer of SDU students, undergraduates, PhD students, or
research-teaching staff to another Kazakh or foreign university/
institution for a semester or academic year, for study or research —
aligned with the Bologna Declaration (1999).

## Types
- **External (international) mobility**: studying/researching abroad
  without extending the standard program duration.
- **Internal (national) mobility**: studying at another Kazakhstani
  university, typically in the same specialty, without extending the
  standard period of study.

## Minimum Requirements
- At least **20 ECTS credits** of coursework/practice must be completed
  during a mobility period (or participation in summer/winter schools,
  or research/practice placements).
- Mobility is based on a bilateral partnership agreement between SDU and
  the host institution.
- Candidate selection follows general criteria (Annex 1), with
  faculty/school-specific criteria possible.

## Administration
The Office of International Relations (OIR) manages information support
for academic mobility (website postings, coordinator notifications,
etc.). Credit recognition on return follows an ECTS-based recalculation
procedure (Section 4 of the regulation).
