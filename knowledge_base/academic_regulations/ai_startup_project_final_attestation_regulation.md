# AI-Startup-Project as Final Attestation (RE_ED_2025_18)

## Purpose
Allows students to complete their final attestation by developing an
AI-powered startup project ("AI-Sana Program") instead of a traditional
thesis/exam, to encourage innovation and digital literacy.

## Key Definitions
- **AI-Startup-Project**: an innovative project using AI technologies to
  create/implement new products or services.
- **FAC**: Final Attestation Commission.

## Process Overview (Section 6)
1. Conditions and requirements for credit awarding (6.1).
2. Submission and review of applications (6.2).
3. Staged implementation of the AI-Sana Program (6.3).
4. Project preparation and defense (6.4).
5. Assessment criteria (6.5).
6. Special provisions for cross-university/cross-school/cross-program
   team participation (6.6).
7. Step-by-step algorithm for implementing the startup project (6.7).

## Related Policies
Built on top of the Academic Integrity Policy, the AI-Use Policy, and the
Research Ethics Policy of SDU University.
