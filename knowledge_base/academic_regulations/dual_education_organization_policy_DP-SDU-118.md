# Dual Education Organization Policy (ДП-SDU-118)

## What Is Dual Education?
A training format combining classroom instruction with mandatory
industrial training and professional internship at a partner enterprise
— the enterprise, the university, and the student share responsibility,
and the enterprise provides a workplace and compensation to the student.

## Key Definitions
- **Dual Education Programme**: developed per faculty/school (e.g. "Beta
  Career," "Edu Career").
- **Dual Education Agreement**: a written contract between student,
  enterprise, and university defining the training conditions.
- **Mentor**: a qualified enterprise employee who supervises the student
  during industrial training.

## Stakeholders (Section 6.1)
The University, participating enterprises/organizations, and students.

## Approval Chain
Developed by the Educational and Methodical Center, reviewed by the
Department of Quality Assurance, discussed at the Administrative Board,
and approved by the Academic Board.
