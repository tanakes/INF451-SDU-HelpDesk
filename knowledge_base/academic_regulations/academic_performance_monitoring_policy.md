# Policy for Monitoring Academic Performance (ДП-SDU-30)

## Purpose
Governs how SDU organizes and controls student academic progress across
current control, intermediate certification, and final certification.

## Types of Control
- **Current control**: ongoing assessment during the semester.
- **Intermediate certification**: midterm-style checkpoints.
- **Final certification**: end-of-course/program assessment, including
  the mandatory state exam in "History of Kazakhstan."

## Grading
- Student achievement is measured via Grade Point Average (GPA).
- The policy distinguishes points that affect GPA from those that do not
  (Sections 6.3–6.4).
