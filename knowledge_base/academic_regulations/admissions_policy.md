# Admissions Policy (ДП-SDU-26)

## Scope
Governs admission requirements and the application process for all
degree levels at SDU University.

## Key Terms
- **UNT**: Unified National Testing
- **EEE**: Entrance English Exam
- **IELTS**: International English Language Testing System (accepted
  alternative for English proficiency)
- **MDEC**: Multidisciplinary Educational Centre
- **GPA**: Grade Point Average

## Process Overview
1. Requirements for applicants vary by program/level (Section 5).
2. Applicants submit an application through the Admissions Department
   (Section 6).
3. Applicants may appeal or request reconsideration of an admissions
   decision (Section 7).
4. Joint educational programs (with partner universities) have
   additional provisions (Section 8).
5. Admission can be cancelled under conditions outlined in Section 9.

## Not Covered Here
This summary does not include the detailed appendix tables of program-
specific entrance requirements, English exam score thresholds, or the
appeal form template. For the complete 25-page policy, dispatch
`admissions_policy.pdf` directly.
