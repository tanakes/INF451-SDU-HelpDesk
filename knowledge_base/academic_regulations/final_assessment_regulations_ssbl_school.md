# Final Assessment Regulations — School of Social Sciences, Business and Law (IN_ED_2026_22)

## Scope
Applies specifically to students in the **School of Social Sciences,
Business and Law (SSBL)** completing their final attestation.

## Final Assessment Forms (Section 4)
1. **Thesis** — including requirements for topic choice, the research
   process, and thesis structure.
2. **Media Project** — with its own structural requirements.
3. **Policy Paper** — with its own structural requirements.
4. **Complex Exam** — an alternative comprehensive exam format.

## Key Topics Covered
- Ethical issues in conducting research (Section 5).
- How to choose a topic and supervisor (Section 6).
- Rights and responsibilities of student and supervisor (Section 7).
- Choosing the proper research method (Section 8).
- Technical requirements and referencing style (Section 9).
- Preparation recommendations for the defense (Section 10).
- Evaluation criteria (Section 11).
- Guidance for online-format defenses (Section 12).
