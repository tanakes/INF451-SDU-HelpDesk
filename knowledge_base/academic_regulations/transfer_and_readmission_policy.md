# Transfer and Readmission Policy (ДП-SDU-33)

## Purpose
Regulates the procedure for transferring to SDU from another institution
and readmission (re-enrollment after dismissal/withdrawal) into higher
and postgraduate programs.

## Structure
- **General Principles** (Section 5)
- **Rules for Transfer and Readmission** between higher education
  institutions (Section 6)
- **English Language Placement Exam** requirement for incoming students
  (Section 7)
- **Appeal and Deferral/Extension of Study Start Date Policy** (Section
  8) — covers how students can appeal decisions or request to delay
  their start date.
