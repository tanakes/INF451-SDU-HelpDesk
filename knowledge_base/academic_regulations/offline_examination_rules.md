# Offline Examination Rules for Students

**Important**: students who violate any of these rules receive an "F"
for the examination.

## Eligibility & Scheduling
- Must be registered for the course (not withdrawn/dropped), with no
  financial or academic debt.
- Exams follow the approved schedule (same day/time); may be scheduled
  on weekends/holidays.
- Only one exam per day (exceptions for retakes/subject differences, via
  application to the instructor/faculty administration).

## In the Exam Room
- Show your student ID or personal ID card and sign the attendance list.
- Sit only in your assigned seat (per the seating plan) — violating this
  results in removal and an "F".
- Follow the examiner/proctor's instructions at all times.
- You may not leave the room during the first 30 minutes after the exam
  starts.
- Books, notes, bags, phones, and electronic devices (smartwatches,
  AirPods, iPads) must be placed in a specified location and turned off.
  **The university is not responsible for their safety.**
- Only pen, pencil, and eraser are allowed by default; reference
  materials, dictionaries, and basic calculators require instructor
  permission. Exchanging items with other students is strictly
  prohibited.
- No talking between students; looking at another student's exam sheet
  or screen is a serious violation resulting in removal and an "F".
- You may ask the instructor questions about the exam content only, with
  permission.

## Lateness
Students more than 30 minutes late are **not allowed** to take the exam.
Students up to 30 minutes late may be allowed (conditions apply — see
the full document for the remaining clause, which was truncated in this
summary).

## Cheating / Phone Use
Immediate removal from the room and an "F" grade.
