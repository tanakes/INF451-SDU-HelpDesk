# Academic Integrity Policy (DP-SDU-28)

## Purpose & Scope
Applies to all SDU students for all assessed academic work: courseworks,
midterm/final attestation works, research/experimental works, practical
works, and final attestation works (diploma work/project, dissertation
thesis/project).

## Key Definitions
- **Plagiarism**: using another author's literary or scientific work (or
  excerpts of it) in one's own work without indicating the author —
  including copying material, adapting it, and using it without citing
  the source.
- **Cheating**: using assistive tools, information, or materials not
  permitted, or helping/attempting to help another student do so.

## Review Process
- All assessed work is subject to review for academic integrity and
  plagiarism, including text-matching software (e.g. Turnitin) and
  proctors for written examinations.
- Students studying under an exchange program follow the host
  university's Academic Integrity Policy.
- Any employee or student who notices an act contrary to academic
  integrity must report it in writing to the appropriate faculty.

## Serious Violations
Submitting fake documents (transcript/diploma/certificate/references),
having another person take an exam on your behalf, or obtaining/
attempting to obtain exam materials in advance are treated as serious
acts contrary to academic integrity (see Appendix C of the policy).

## Appeal
Students may appeal a decision made regarding an academic integrity case
(Section 5 of the policy).
