# Online Examination Rules for Students

## Eligibility
To take an online exam, students must:
- Be registered for the course (not withdrawn/dropped).
- Have no financial or academic debt.

## Scheduling
- Exams follow the approved schedule; faculty/school may schedule exams
  on weekends/holidays.
- Only **one exam per day** is allowed (except for retakes/subject
  differences — these require an application to the course instructor
  and faculty administration if they coincide with a main exam date).

## Technical Setup
- Taking the exam via **mobile phone is prohibited**.
- Students must verify their laptop/computer, internet connection, and
  exam platform in advance.
- **Moodle exams**: automatic proctoring system is used.
- **Webex synchronous exams**: no phone calls, browsing other pages, or
  talking to others in the room.
- **Webex oral exams**: connect 15 minutes before your assigned time.

## Prohibited During Exam
Books, notes, notebooks, bags, mobile phones, and electronic devices
(smartwatches, AirPods, tablets) must not be near the computer, unless
the instructor grants an exception (e.g. calculator, dictionary).

## Violations & Appeals
- If "Regulation for Online Proctoring" violations are detected, exam
  results are reviewed again.
- Technical problems preventing exam completion: student may apply to
  resit within 1 working day (application to instructor and faculty
  administration).
- Appeals are submitted to the faculty/school administration.
