# Academic Leave Regulation (ДП-SDU-34)

## Grounds for Academic Leave
A student may be granted academic leave on these grounds:
1. **Medical reasons** — based on form 035-1/U primary medical
   documentation and a conclusion from the Medical Advisory Commission
   (MAC) at an outpatient-polyclinic organization, for the duration
   specified.
2. **Tuberculosis treatment** — based on a decision of the Centralized
   Medical Advisory Commission of the Tuberculosis Organization (CMACTO),
   up to 12–36 months.
3. **Military service summons** — based on a conscription notice.
4. **Childbirth/adoption** — academic leave of 12–36 months for a child
   born or adopted before the child reaches age three.

## How to Apply
The student submits to the SDU Rector an application (per Annex 1 of the
Regulation), an identity document, and supporting documents matching the
grounds above. Medical certificates must first be authenticated by the
SDU medical center staff before being forwarded. Original documents are
verified against copies and returned to the applicant.
