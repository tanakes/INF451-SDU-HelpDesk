# Office Hours — Guidelines for Students

## What Are Office Hours?
Time slots professors allocate for students to book appointments to
better understand course content or get clarification on
assessment/feedback — an informal meeting to ask questions.

## Important Rule
**Students must not contact teachers via personal mobile numbers or
social media.** All teacher-student communication must go through:
- Corporate email,
- Office Hours, or
- The Moodle platform.

## How & When
- Office Hours can be held **offline or online**, per instructor
  preference.
- Each instructor schedules their own Office Hours (weekly or several
  times per semester) — published in the syllabus and on Moodle.

## Why Attend
- Clarify course content and assignments
- Get study tips
- Discuss grades or review past exams/papers
- Discuss expectations for upcoming assignments

## If You Can't Attend the Scheduled Time
- **By email**: introduce yourself (name, surname, ID, course, year),
  explain why you can't attend, propose alternative times, thank the
  professor.
- **In person**: same approach — introduce yourself, explain, propose
  alternatives.
