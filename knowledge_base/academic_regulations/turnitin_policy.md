# Turnitin Policy (ДП-SDU-51)

## Purpose
Guidelines for using Turnitin (plagiarism-detection and originality-
checking service) at SDU, to promote academic integrity.

## Key Facts
- SDU has a license contract with Turnitin.
- **Use of Turnitin is NOT mandatory**, but students and instructors are
  encouraged to use its originality report to improve academic integrity
  and work quality.
- Based on the SDU Academic Integrity Policy (2020).

## Key Definitions
- **Plagiarism**: possession/use of another author's work or excerpts
  without attribution.
- **Direct plagiarism**: word-for-word copying without quotation marks,
  presented as one's own.
- **Self-plagiarism (auto-plagiarism)**: reusing one's own prior
  scientific work without disclosure.

## Similarity Factors
The policy distinguishes **acceptable** vs **unacceptable** similarity
factors (Section 5) — e.g. properly quoted/cited material vs. uncredited
copying.

## Consequences
Academic integrity violations detected via Turnitin follow the
consequences outlined in Section 6 of this policy (cross-referenced with
the Academic Integrity Policy).
