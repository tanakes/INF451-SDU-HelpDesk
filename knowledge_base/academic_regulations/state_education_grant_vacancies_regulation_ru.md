# Vacant State Education Grant Places Regulation (ДП-SDU-62) — in Kazakh

## What This Document Covers
Defines the procedure for awarding "vacated" state education grants
(grants that became available, e.g. when a grant-holder leaves or is
dismissed) to fee-paying bachelor's/master's students on a competitive
basis.

## Key Points
- Competition is based on GPA from the most recent intermediate
  certification.
- **Minimum GPA required: 2.5**. In case of a tie, priority goes to
  students with more "A/A-" (excellent) grades, then "B+/B/B-/C+" (good)
  grades.
- Announcements are posted on the university's education portal,
  specifying available grant slots by specialty and course year.
- Application periods: during summer and winter holidays, specifically
  **31 July – 4 August** and **19–24 January** each year.
- Students apply via a formal request to the Rector, submitted through
  the Student Service Center (SSC@sdu.edu.kz).
- The Scientific Council reviews and approves the final candidate list
  based on SSC's recommendation.
