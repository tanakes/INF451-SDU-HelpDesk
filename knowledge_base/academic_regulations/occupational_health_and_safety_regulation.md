# Chemistry Laboratory Health & Safety Regulation (ДП-SDU-Ф9)

## Scope
Governs health and safety procedures specifically for the **Teaching
Chemistry Laboratory** (supporting the Chemistry-Biology bachelor's
program 6B01502 and the Chemistry Education master's program 7M01502),
particularly when working with regulated precursor chemicals.

## Governance
- The Laboratory operates under the Vice-Rector for Academic Affairs,
  the Dean of the Faculty of Education and Humanities, and the Head of
  the Department of Natural Sciences.
- Headed by a Laboratory Head, appointed by the Rector on the Dean's
  recommendation, requiring higher professional education and 3+ years
  of relevant lab experience.

## Structure
- Safety requirements for storage/placement of chemical reagents and
  equipment (4.1)
- Safety measures for handling laboratory glassware (4.2)
- Safety requirements for conducting chemical experiments (4.3)
- Personal protective equipment requirements (4.4)

## Who This Affects
Primarily relevant to students and staff in Chemistry-Biology or
Chemistry Education programs who use this specific laboratory — not a
general-population student document.

## Not Covered Here
This summary does not reproduce the detailed safety instructions
(31-page document with 8 appendices). For complete details, dispatch
`occupational_health_and_safety_regulation.pdf` directly, or refer
students to the Department of Natural Sciences.
