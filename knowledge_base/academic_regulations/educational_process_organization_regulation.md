# Educational Process Organization — Credit Technology (ДП-SDU-01)

## Purpose
Establishes the procedure for organizing the entire educational process
at SDU under the Credit Technology of Education (CTE) model — the
foundational regulation underlying course registration, ECTS credit
structure, and academic mobility rules.

## Structure
- **Section 1 — General Provisions**: foundational definitions and
  regulatory references for the CTE system.
- **Section 2 — Procedure for Organizing the Educational Process**:
  - Educational Programs and Curricula under CTE
  - The educational process itself (course structure, scheduling)
  - Student academic mobility, including international credit mobility
  - Double-degree and joint educational program procedures

## Relationship to Other Documents
This is the master regulation that the Academic Calendar
(`knowledge_base/registration/academic_calendar.md`), Academic Mobility
Regulation, and Tuition Fees Regulation all operate under — it defines
what "credit technology," "ECTS," and "educational program" mean in the
SDU context.
