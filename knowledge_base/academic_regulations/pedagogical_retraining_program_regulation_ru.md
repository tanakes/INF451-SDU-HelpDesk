# Pedagogical Retraining Program Rules (АП-SDU-115) — in Russian

## What This Document Covers
Rules for enrolling in SDU's **Pedagogical Retraining Program** —
designed for people with professional (non-pedagogical) education who
are starting a teaching career and need pedagogical qualification.

## Program Structure
- **Format**: in-person with distance-learning technology elements.
- **Components**: Pedagogy module (28 ECTS) + Specialization module (9
  ECTS) + Practicum (10 ECTS) = **47 ECTS total**.
- **Duration**: 6 months.

## Admission Requirements
- Minimum of 4 specialization subjects on the applicant's diploma
  transcript.
- Minimum of 3 registered participants to open a group for a given
  specialty.
- Required documents: application to the Rector, ID copy, notarized
  copy of education diploma, one 3×4cm photo, medical certificate (form
  075/U).

## Payment & Completion
- A contract is signed after enrollment; tuition payment is due per the
  contract schedule.
- Upon passing all subject exams, a certificate is issued.
- If a subject score is below 50 points, the student must pay for and
  retake that subject's credits.
- Retakes allowed during study and up to 3 months after completion.
- Student records are deleted from the university database 9 months
  after program start (6 months of study + 3 months grace period).
