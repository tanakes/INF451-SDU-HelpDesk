# Migration Policy Regulation (ДП-SDU-43) — in Russian

## What This Document Covers
Procedures for inviting, registering, and supporting **foreign
citizens** (students and employees) at SDU — primarily relevant to
international students and staff.

## Key Points
- **Visa category C9** (study visa): invitation letters are issued only
  based on a confirmation letter from the relevant department head or
  admissions committee — not directly to the applicant. Recommended
  timing: December–January for spring intake, June–July for fall intake.
- **Visa category C3** (work visa): similar invitation process for
  foreign employees, coordinated with the HR Department.
- Required documents for an invitation: scanned passport copy, home
  address, intended visa pickup location (embassy/consulate city), and
  the faculty/course the applicant is joining.
- Invitation approval by the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MIA) of
  Kazakhstan takes about **5 working days**.
- The visa is valid for **1 year**, affixed to the passport, and becomes
  active only **14 days after** the invitation is submitted.
- **Mandatory registration**: foreign citizens must register with the
  Office of International Relations (room E110) **within 1 working day**
  of crossing the Kazakhstan border.
- The document also covers passport registration procedures and
  document nostrification (degree recognition) requirements.
