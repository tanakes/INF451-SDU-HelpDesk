# LMS Moodle Usage Policy (ДП-SDU-83)

## Purpose
Clarifies conditions for using and administering Moodle (moodle.sdu.edu.kz),
SDU's **only** official Learning Management System — used for the entire
academic process regardless of delivery mode (on-campus/distance/blended).

## Key Rules
- **Login**: Users must log in with their own SDU account (name.surname
  or student ID) — sharing accounts is not allowed.
- **Enrollment keys**: Instructors should not disclose course enrollment
  keys in group chats to prevent unauthorized access.
- **Auditing**: All Moodle activity (course access, forum posts,
  assignment submissions) is logged and may be used as evidence in
  academic integrity or conduct matters.
- **Content distribution**: Instructors acknowledge that materials
  uploaded to Moodle are available for download and cannot guarantee
  against redistribution.

## Scope
Applies university-wide to teaching staff and administration; students
are users of the system under these same security principles.
