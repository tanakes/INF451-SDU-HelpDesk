# Tuition Fees Regulation (ДП-SDU-22)

This is the **official regulation** behind the public tuition-fees page
summarized in `knowledge_base/tuition/tuition_fees.md`. It answers detail
questions (discounts, SDU grants) that the public page does not cover.

## Discounts (Section 3)
- **Types & amounts** of tuition discounts/benefits are approved yearly
  by the Board of Finance (announced before May each year); listed in
  Annex 1 of the regulation.
- **Application window**: students must apply at the start of the
  semester (at least 10 working days before course-registration begins
  per the academic calendar), or during the admissions document period
  for new applicants.
- **First-year-only restriction**: discount applications for academic
  *achievement* can only be made during the student's **first academic
  year** — after that, achievement-based discounts cannot be newly
  applied for (holders of *social* benefits are exempt from this
  restriction). Students transferring in or in retraining programs are
  only eligible for social benefits.
- **Late application grace period**: if a student misses the normal
  deadline, they can still apply until the end of **week 7** of the
  current term — but the discount only takes effect from the *next*
  term.
- **"Best student in speciality" discount**: students on a fee basis
  with the highest GPA in their speciality (tracked separately per
  academic year level) can receive a **50% discount** for the following
  academic year, conditional on maintaining a GPA of at least 3.0. This
  is separate from the SDU Grant.
- **Discount withdrawal conditions**: GPA dropping below 3.0, changing
  academic standing, transferring to another institution, or expulsion
  all result in losing the discount. A student can regain a lost
  discount if their annual GPA returns to at least 3.0.

## SDU Grant (Section 4)
A separate benefit category from discounts — holders who receive a full
grant or a 50% discount through this program have specific responsibilities
(Section 5) and must return the discounted amount if those responsibilities
are violated.

## Contact
Students can address tuition fee or personal-account questions via the
university's internal information system or by emailing
**accounting@sdu.edu.kz**.
