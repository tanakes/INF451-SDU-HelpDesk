# AI Tools Use in Academic Activities (ДП-SDU-128)

## Purpose
Defines the procedure for using AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot)
in academic activities by faculty, staff, and students — building on the
university's broader "Artificial Intelligence Policy."

## Scope
Applies to all educational programs, research, and academic activities
at SDU, covering teaching staff, students, and anyone else involved in
academic work.

## Key Rules (Section 5)
- **5.1 Rules for using AI** in the academic process.
- **5.2 Showing the use of AI** — students/staff must disclose AI usage
  where required.
- **5.3 Cases where AI use is prohibited** (specific prohibited contexts
  are listed in the full document).
- **5.4 Teacher support** for appropriate AI integration into coursework.

## Enforcement
- Section 6 covers control/monitoring of AI use.
- Section 7 covers sanctions for unethical AI use.
- Section 8 covers training and support resources.
- Section 9 covers information security and personal data protection
  requirements when using AI tools.

## Related Policies
References the Law "On Education," the Law "On Personal Data and Its
Protection," the Law "On Informatization," the AI Policy, Information
Security Policy, and Academic Integrity Policy.

## Not Covered Here
This summary does not list every prohibited AI-use scenario or exact
sanction tiers. For the complete regulation, dispatch
`ai_tools_use_in_academic_activities_regulation.pdf` directly.
