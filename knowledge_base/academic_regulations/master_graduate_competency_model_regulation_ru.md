# Master's Graduate Competency Model Regulation (ДП-SDU-86) — in Russian

## What This Document Covers
The master's-level counterpart to the bachelor's competency model
regulation — defines expected competencies for SDU master's graduates.

## Key Points
- Developed by the graduating department and the educational program
  coordinator, based on state education standards.
- A master's degree is awarded after completing the Bologna Process's
  "second cycle" of higher education.
- In Kazakhstan, master's programs follow two tracks:
  - **Scientific-pedagogical track**: 2 years, prepares researchers and
    academic staff for higher/postgraduate education and research.
  - **Profile (professional) track**: minimum 1 year, prepares
    specialists with deep professional training (non-research focused).
