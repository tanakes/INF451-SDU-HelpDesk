# Bachelor's Graduate Competency Model Regulation (ДП-SDU-81) — in Russian

## What This Document Covers
Internal regulatory document defining the "competency model" each SDU
bachelor's program graduate must achieve by graduation — a formal
description of expected knowledge, skills, and competencies tied to the
Bologna Process and labor-market requirements.

## Key Points
- Each specialty's competency model is developed by the "graduating
  department" (выпускающая кафедра), based on state education standards
  (ГОСО) and relevant industry requirements.
- The model includes: program goals/objectives, the professional
  activity profile of the graduate (field, objects, types, and tasks of
  professional activity), and competency descriptors (Appendix 1).
- Program goals are organized into 3 course cycles:
  - **General education disciplines (ООД)**: social-humanitarian
    education, history of Kazakhstan, ICT, languages.
  - **Base disciplines (БД)**: natural science, technical, and economic
    foundations.
  - **Specialization disciplines (ПД)**: deep theoretical/practical
    knowledge in the student's specialty.
