# Non-Formal Education Regulation (ДП-SDU-109)

## What Is Non-Formal Education?
Education/training provided outside a formal educational institution's
usual terms and mode of study, that still issues a certificate confirming
learning outcomes (per Article 37 of the Law "On Education").

## How Credit Recognition Works
- Learning outcomes from non-formal education can be equated to
  **elective component disciplines** only (not thesis, traineeship, or
  internship courses).
- A discipline not yet registered in the student's curriculum, or a
  retake/outdated discipline, is equated based on the School's decision.
- Recognition only happens within the time limits set by the Academic
  Calendar.

## Recognition Commission
- Each department establishes a Commission (odd number of members: at
  least 3 or 5) to review and approve recognition requests.
- A Chairman and Deputy Chairman are elected by majority vote; a
  non-member specialist serves as secretary.
- Decisions are documented in minutes, specifying discipline name, ECTS
  volume, and grade (Appendix 1).
