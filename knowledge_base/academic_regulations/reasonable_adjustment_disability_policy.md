# Reasonable Adjustment for Students with Disability (ДП-SDU-122)

## Purpose
Articulates SDU's commitment to providing reasonable adjustments for
students with Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND), to
ensure equal access and inclusion in academic life.

## Scope
Applies to all current students (undergraduate and graduate) defined as
having SEND, regardless of nationality, fee status, or residence.

## Legal Basis
Aligned with:
- The Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Education" (2007, No. 319-III)
- The national Roadmap for Inclusive Education Development 2023–2025
- SDU's Equality, Diversity, and Inclusivity Policy

## Structure
- **Responsibilities** (Section 5) — who is responsible for implementing
  adjustments (includes the Educational and Methodical Centre, EMC).
- **Implementation Framework** (Section 6)
- **Confidentiality** (Section 7) — how SEND information is protected.
- **Appeals, Complaints, and Mitigation** (Section 8) — process if a
  student disagrees with an adjustment decision.

## Not Covered Here
This summary does not list the specific types of adjustments available
(e.g. extended exam time, accessible materials) — these are detailed
further in the body of the document. For complete details, dispatch
`reasonable_adjustment_disability_policy.pdf` directly, or refer the
student to the Inclusion Support Services office.
