# Scholarship and Grant Programs Regulation (ДП-SDU-66)

## Purpose
Supports socially active students, high academic performers, and
students from low-income families through scholarships and internal
grants.

## Structure
- **Conditions and Procedure for Awarding** (Section 6)
- **Types of Grants Covered by the Grant Office** (Section 7)
- **Competition Procedure and Format** (Section 8) — how students apply
  and compete for grants.
- **Grounds for Termination** of scholarship/grant payments (Section 9)
  — e.g. GPA drops, disciplinary issues, change of status.

## Relevant Office
The **Grant Office** administers these programs and the competition
process.

## Not Covered Here
This summary does not include the exact grant amounts, specific
eligibility GPA thresholds, or application form templates (3 appendices
in the original). For complete details, dispatch
`scholarship_and_grant_programs_regulation.pdf` directly, or refer the
student to the Grant Office.
