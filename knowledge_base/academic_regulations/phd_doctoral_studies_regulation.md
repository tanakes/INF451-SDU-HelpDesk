# PhD/Master's Scientific Internship Guidelines (ДП-SDU-73)

## Purpose
Governs the organization of scientific (research) internships for
postgraduate students — Master's and PhD doctoral students — as part of
SDU's Quality Management System (QMS).

## Who's Responsible
- Master's program directors organize internships for Master's students
  (with the Department of International Relations for foreign
  internships).
- The internship is part of the student's individual Master's Scientific
  Research/Experimental Research Work (MSR/MERW) plan.

## Structure
- **6.1 Organization of scientific internships in the Master's program**
- **6.2 Organization of internships in MBA and EMBA Master's programs**
  (different rules apply to professional/business programs)
- **6.4 Conducting internships during restrictive measures** (pandemic-
  era provisions, likely still relevant for remote/hybrid cases)
- **6.5 Research internship fees** — internships may carry a fee.

## Not Covered Here
This summary does not include the exact internship fee amounts or
MBA/EMBA-specific internship requirements. For complete details,
dispatch `phd_doctoral_studies_regulation.pdf` directly.
