# Master's Research Practice Guidelines (Kazakh) — Protocol No. 6, 2023

## What This Document Covers
Guidelines for organizing and conducting the mandatory **research
practice** component for master's students — a required part of
deepening theoretical/methodological training and preparing the
Master's dissertation.

## Key Points
- Research practice is organized after completing the relevant specialized
  course cycle or theoretical preparation.
- Its content is tied to the student's dissertation research topic.
- Students must: analyze/synthesize scientific information on their
  topic, conduct theoretical/practical research, verify the reliability
  of results, compare their findings with domestic/international
  analogues, and assess scientific/practical significance.
- **Practice duration**: calculated as 1 ECTS = 1 week, per the academic
  calendar and curriculum.
- Students may independently find a practice placement organization if
  it fits their program profile and dissertation topic; a formal
  agreement must be signed no later than 1 month before the academic
  year starts.
- The faculty/school assigns a practice supervisor who develops the
  practice program, organizes/monitors it, and conducts the student's
  final assessment.
