# Assessment Policy (ДП-SDU-96)

## Purpose
Establishes unified principles for fair, transparent, and reliable
measurement of learning outcomes across all SDU programs (bachelor's,
master's, doctoral) and all formative/summative assessments.

## Key Definitions
- **Formative Assessment**: ongoing evaluation during a course to gauge
  understanding and progress.
- **Summative Assessment**: evaluation at the end of an instructional
  period (module, course, semester, program).
- **External Examiner**: an independent examiner from outside SDU.
- **Moderation**: process of verifying assessment schemes/grading for
  consistency and fairness.
- **Proctor**: a person who supervises/monitors students during exams.

## Core Principles (Section 5.2)
- **Transparency**: assessment components must be clear and accessible.
- **Fairness**: equitable application of criteria, removing barriers.
- **Validity**: assessment matches intended learning outcomes.
- **Reliability**: judgments are accurate, verifiable, and consistent.
- **Integrity**: mechanisms exist to address breaches and appeals.

## Scope of Sections
- Administration of Assessment (6)
- Examination Assessment and Moderation (7)
- Feedback on Assessment (8)
- Academic Dismissal grounds (9)
- Normal Distribution of Grades / Bell Curve (10)
