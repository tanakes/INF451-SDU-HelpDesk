# Transcripts & Student Service Center (SSC) Documents

## Current Student Services

### Academic Transcripts
An official transcript is the university's certified statement of your
academic record. Transcripts are issued upon request.
- Bring your student ID to the SSC office during working days.
- **Ready within 48 hours** after the request.

### Enrollment Verification for Military Service
For male Kazakhstani residents aged 18–27, to postpone military service
due to academic enrollment.
- Bring your student ID.
- **Ready within 48 hours** after the request.

### Enrollment Verification for Social Payment
For social payment applications. Several payment types exist (e.g. No.4,
No.6, No.2.1, No.3) — confirm the exact type needed via SCSP (ГЦВП) before
requesting.
- Bring your student ID.
- **Ready within 48 hours** after the request.

### Enrollment Verification (General)
For banks, schools, employers, or parents' employers.
- Bring your student ID.
- **Ready within 48 hours** after the request.

### Student ID Reissue (Paid)
If lost, damaged, or malfunctioning.
- Cost: **2,000 KZT**
- **Ready within 24 hours** after the request.

### Change of Major
Requires a written request submitted to the SSC office at least **2 weeks
prior to the start of the next semester** (exact dates in the Academic
Calendar). Required info on the form: student name/surname, student ID,
current major, and new major.
- **Important**: if a student has any retakes ("F") during the first
  semester of the first academic year, all unfulfilled credits must be
  covered before transferring. Fall-semester courses are typically not
  offered in spring; retakes may need to wait until summer or the
  following fall.

### Exchange Student Transcript
Transcripts for exchange students are usually delivered to SDU 1–2 months
after the exchange program ends. This can delay monthly stipend payments
for government scholarship holders — contact the International Relations
Office (IRO) with questions.

### Monthly Governmental Stipend
Paid to state scholarship holders. Contact the SSC office with questions.

### Diploma Issues
The diploma issuance date is published in the Academic Calendar. No
diplomas are issued before that date.

### Change of Name or Surname
Visit the SSC office with original supporting documents in a timely
manner. Late notification may cause problems with academic history
records.

## Alumni Services

### Archival Information
Confirms graduation from SDU and that the language of instruction was
English. Contact SSC via email (ssc@sdu.edu.kz) with your Diploma,
Transcript, and passport/ID attached.
- **Ready within 10 business days** after the request.

### Diploma Duplicate
Processed through the electronic Government portal (egov.kz) or CSC
(ЦОН) service centers — the university does not accept direct requests
for this. Expected processing time: **1 month**.

## Not Covered Here
This file does not contain petition-form PDFs or downloadable templates
for the services above (e.g. change-of-major request forms) — the public
SDU website does not publish these as standalone downloadable files. If a
student asks for a specific form/template, the bot should state this
information is not in the FAQ and direct them to visit the Student
Service Center (SSC) in Block B in person.
