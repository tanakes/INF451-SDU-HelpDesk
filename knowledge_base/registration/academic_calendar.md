# Course Registration & Academic Calendar (2026–2027)

## Fall Semester 2026 — Key Dates (1st-year students)
- **26.08.2026 – 28.08.2026**: Orientation week
- **26.08.2026 – 01.09.2026**: Course registration on my.sdu.edu.kz
- **01.09.2026**: Day of Knowledge / Classes start
- **02.09.2026 – 07.09.2026**: Additional course openings (extra request/meet) on pms.sdu.edu.kz
- **07.09.2026 – 12.09.2026**: Additional course registration for transferred/newly admitted students (add/drop)
- **14.09.2026 – 18.09.2026**: Late course registration (add only) for fee-basis students
- **21.09.2026 – 16.10.2026**: Course withdrawal, Phase 1 (early withdraw)
- **19.10.2026 – 11.12.2026**: Course withdrawal, Phase 2 (late withdraw)
- **12.12.2026**: Classes end
- **14.12.2026 – 26.12.2026**: Winter final examinations
- **27.12.2026 – 17.01.2027**: Winter holidays

## Fall Semester 2026 — Key Dates (2nd–4th year students / PhD)
- **03.08.2026**: Final day to submit tuition payment requests for the semester
- **10.08.2026 – 21.08.2026**: Course registration window on my.sdu.edu.kz, staggered by earned ECTS:
  - 10–21 Aug: students with >170 ECTS (Bachelor), >60 ECTS (Master's), >120 ECTS (PhD)
  - 13–21 Aug: students with >110 ECTS (Bachelor), >60 ECTS (PhD)
  - 18–21 Aug: students with >50 ECTS
  - Registration opens 00:00 and closes 23:59 on the final day of each window.
- Same withdrawal/exam/holiday schedule as above applies to all years.

## Spring Semester 2027 — Key Dates
- **01.12.2026 – 10.01.2027**: Acceptance of applications to pay for the semester
- **08.01.2027 – 16.01.2027**: Course registration on my.sdu.edu.kz (staggered by ECTS, same tiered pattern as fall)
- **18.01.2027**: Classes start
- **18.01.2027 – 22.01.2027**: Additional course openings
- **25.01.2027 – 29.01.2027**: Additional registration (add/drop) for transferred/re-enrolled students
- **08.02.2027 – 05.03.2027**: Course withdrawal, Phase 1 (early)
- **08.03.2027 – 30.04.2027**: Course withdrawal, Phase 2 (late)
- **30.04.2027**: Classes end
- **04.05.2027 – 18.05.2027**: Spring final examinations
- **19.05.2027 – 20.06.2027**: Summer holidays

## Summer Semester 2027 — Key Dates
- **19.05.2027 – 11.06.2027**: Acceptance of applications to pay for the summer semester
- **19.05.2027 – 04.06.2027**: Wish-list application period for summer course openings
- **14.06.2027 – 16.06.2027**: Course registration for the summer semester
- **21.06.2027 – 23.07.2027**: Summer semester instruction period
- **26.07.2027 – 28.07.2027**: Summer semester examination period

## Retake Examinations (FX) — Fall/Winter example
- **28.12.2026 – 05.01.2027**: Application to retake exam (FX) via my.sdu.edu.kz Grade List (payment required in advance)
- **11.01.2027 – 13.01.2027**: Retake examination period and grade submission
- **14.01.2027 – 15.01.2027**: Appeal Committee working days for retake exams

## National Holidays Observed in the Calendar
Republic Day (25.10, observed 26.10.2026), Independence Day (16.12.2026),
New Year (01–02.01.2027), Christmas (07.01.2027), International Women's
Day (08.03.2027), Nauryz (21–24.03.2027), Unity Day (01.05, observed
03.05.2027), Defender of the Fatherland Day (07.05.2027), Victory Day
(09.05, observed 10.05.2027).

## Scope Note
This file covers the student-facing registration/add-drop/withdrawal/exam
calendar only. Faculty, School, and SSC administrative deadlines (syllabus
approval, advisor assignment, scholarship order issuance, etc.) exist in
the same calendar but are out of scope for student-facing FAQ answers —
refer students to the Academic Calendar page link if they ask about those.
