1. Project Purpose & Justification

SDU students currently rely on manual inquiries to university staff for routine academic information — tuition deadlines, course registration windows, dormitory procedures, and transcript requests. This creates repetitive administrative workload for staff and delays for students seeking simple, factual answers.

Project Goal: To develop an AI University Help Desk and Virtual Assistant as a Telegram-based helpdesk bot that provides SDU university students with instant access to academic information, university services, and administrative support in Kazakh, Russian, and English. The bot aims to reduce student dependency on manual inquiries to university staff by offering AI-powered responses to frequently asked questions, automated deadline reminders, and seamless escalation to human advisors when needed, ultimately improving the overall student experience and reducing administrative workload.

The project addresses this by building an AI agent, accessible through Telegram (the messaging app students already use daily), that answers common questions instantly, escalates complex cases to human advisors, and gives university staff a maintainable knowledge base and analytics layer.

2. Project Objectives & Success Criteria

2.1 Objectives

  • Provide students with 24/7 conversational access to academic information via Telegram, without requiring a separate app or portal login.
  • Ground every AI response in verified, official SDU documents to eliminate hallucinated or outdated information.
  • Reduce the volume of routine inquiries handled manually by university advisors.
  • Support Kazakh, Russian, and English so the bot serves the full student population (multilingual support planned for Sprint 3).
  • Provide advisors with tools to maintain the knowledge base, review escalations, and monitor inquiry trends without needing to modify code.

3. Scope

3.1 In Scope

  • A Telegram bot channel built on Buda's native Channel Gateway (no self-hosted server).
  • A structured 4-topic FAQ menu with quick keyword shortcuts (Tuition Fees, Course Add/Drop, Dormitory, Transcripts).
  • A university knowledge base of official SDU documents grounding all bot answers.
  • Direct delivery of official links and PDF guides (academic calendars, petition forms) inside the Telegram chat.
  • Multilingual understanding in Kazakh, Russian, and English, including typo/colloquial tolerance.
  • Automatic classification of incoming questions into tracked categories.
  • Role-based access control separating Student and Advisor capabilities.
  • Student profile context (major, year of study) for tailored answers.
  • Escalation workflow to human advisors with ticket tracking.
  • Advisor-facing knowledge base maintenance tools and student feedback/rating collection.
  • Scheduled deadline reminders and analytics/reporting for administration.

3.2 Out of Scope

  • A standalone web or mobile application — Sprint 1–7 scope is Telegram-only (a web version is logged only as a future enhancement idea).
  • Automated processing of payments, enrollment, or any transactional university system changes.
  • Any storage or handling of student passwords, IIN, or bank card details.
  • Unverified or non-official information sources in the knowledge base.
